# Code of conduct

Project discussions should make it possible to review technical work without
personal hostility. Treat contributors and users respectfully, explain criticism
with evidence, and accept correction. People of different backgrounds and levels
of experience are welcome.

Harassment, discriminatory abuse, threats, sexualized conduct, deliberate exposure
of private information, and repeated personal attacks are unacceptable. Do not
post credentials, private telemetry or material that endangers another person.
The same expectations apply in issues, pull requests and other project spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, close disruptive discussions
or restrict participation. Where practical, explain the reason and give the
person a chance to correct the behavior. Immediate protective action may be
necessary for threats or exposure of private information.

To report a conduct concern, request a private contact channel from the AegisTrace
maintainer through an issue without publishing incident details or personal data.
Keep the report factual and share only what is needed. Vulnerability reports use
the separate [security reporting channel](SECURITY.md).
